In-App Purchases & Restore

To restore previous purchases (Remove Ads, Founder's Cabinet, Scenario Packs):

  • Open Settings from the top bar in the game.
  • Tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Google Play or Apple ID account you used for the purchase.
  • Purchases are tied to your store account and restore across reinstallations.

Gameplay & Offline Play

Middle East 2030 is fully playable offline. Your progress, terms, and archive front pages are saved locally on your device. Game Center and Play Games leaderboards update when you are connected to the internet.
