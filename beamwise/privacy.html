Privacy Policy

Beamwise: Light Puzzle (iOS and Android) · Effective November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the Beamwise app ("the game", "we") uses and why. The game is made by aviisr games (Avi Israeli Rozin, Israel). Questions: aviisr@aviisrgames.com

Information the game keeps on your device

There are no accounts and no sign-in, and the game works offline. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers for the game and we do not use an analytics SDK, so we do not receive your progress, your settings or how you play. Deleting the game deletes this information.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob mediation. AdMob works with these ad partners, and each one is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Liftoff Monetize (Vungle): liftoff.ai/privacy-policy
  • Meta Audience Network: facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • InMobi: inmobi.com/privacy-policy
  • Chartboost: chartboost.com/privacy
  • Index Exchange (bids through Google): indexexchange.com/privacy

To show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, the ad SDKs and partners may collect: your device's advertising identifier (the advertising ID on Android, and the IDFA on iPhone and iPad only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information (such as model, operating system, language and app version), how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information. We do not receive this data about you; it goes to the ad providers.

Consent and your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and you can change your choices at any time in Settings > Privacy options. On iPhone and iPad, the game also shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, the game works exactly the same, and ads are shown without the advertising identifier. You can also reset or turn off your advertising identifier in your device settings (on iOS, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

Purchases

In-app purchases (Remove Automatic Ads and the Night Sky Pack) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The game receives a purchase confirmation from the store so it can unlock what you bought. Purchases are restored through the store.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to include. We use it only to answer you and fix problems, and we delete it when it is no longer needed or when you ask. Crash and install statistics are provided to us by Apple (App Store Connect) and Google (Google Play Console) in aggregate form.

Children

Beamwise is a general-audience puzzle game for players 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Retention

Progress and settings stay on your device until you delete the game. We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you. Ad providers keep data under their own retention rules, described in the policies linked above.

Your choices and rights

You can change your ad consent choices in Settings, decline tracking on iPhone and iPad, reset or turn off your advertising identifier in your device settings, and delete all data on your device by deleting the game. People in the EU, the UK and Switzerland (GDPR), California (CCPA/CPRA), Israel and other regions have rights under their local privacy laws, such as access, correction, deletion and objecting to personalized advertising. Because we hold no account data about you, most requests about ad data must go to the ad provider concerned, using the policies above. To ask us about anything we hold, or to exercise a right, email aviisr@aviisrgames.com, and we will honor it.

Changes

If the game's use of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
