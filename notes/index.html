About these notes

We make small mobile games, and we like to explain how they work. These notes are where we do that in more detail than a store listing allows. Each one covers a real decision we made, what we tried, and what we would tell someone building something similar.

They are written by the people who made the games, in our own words. We do not repost listing copy, and we try to say plainly when something did not work the first time. Where a note describes a number or a rule, it describes what the game actually does.

The notes are short and independent, so you can read any one without the others. Some are about puzzles and the programs that check them. Others are about game design, such as why a political strategy game is set in an invented year. If you would like a note on something specific, or you spot a mistake, write to aviisr@aviisrgames.com.
