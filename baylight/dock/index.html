How to Play the Depot Puzzle

  • Only the front parcel on the conveyor belt plays: Tap any open dock bay (or press keys 1, 2, 3, 4) that is either empty or holds that same color.
  • 3 of a kind ships the truck: The 3rd parcel completes the load and ships the truck, immediately clearing the bay.
  • Locked Lanes (Dock 5): Certain docks feature locked bays (🔒) that open only after you successfully ship your first truck.
  • The Pallet (Dock 6): When no bay has room, park the front parcel on the pallet (press P). Whenever a bay of that color is placed or freed, the parked parcel jumps in automatically!
  • Colorblind Symbols: Blue [≡], Yellow [●], Red [✚], Green [◎], Purple [╱], Orange [■].
