Privacy Policy

Pagetease (Android and iOS) · Effective November 27, 2026 (updated November 27, 2026)

Pagetease is an illustrated romance reading app for adults, published by aviisr games. This policy explains what the app does with information. Questions: aviisr@aviisrgames.com

This policy covers version 1.0.1 and later. Version 1.0.0 shows no ads, has no purchases and sends no analytics or crash reports.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. Your reading progress, library and settings stay on your device.
  • We never send the title, genre or content of what you read to anyone: not to advertisers, not to analytics, not to us.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob and Unity Ads. They collect device and ad data to show and measure ads.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports go to Google Firebase. You can turn each one off in Profile.
  • Pagetease is for adults (18+). The app checks your device's age signal on the device and never stores or sends it.

What stays on your device

Your reading progress, saved stories, the chapters you have unlocked, text and theme settings, and your privacy choices are stored only on your device. You can clear them at any time in Profile > Delete local data, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising

Chapters 1 to 3 of every story are free. To unlock more chapters you can choose to watch a short ad (a rewarded ad that plays only when you tap to watch it), or buy the Founder's Pass, which removes ads. Ads are delivered through Google AdMob (Google LLC), which also selects ads from Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Ads are requested as non-personalized and without content keywords, so no information about what you read is passed to advertisers.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google AdMob, Unity Ads and the ad partners they work with may collect: device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier where available, and identifiers the ad network assigns to your device or app installation), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version), how you interact with ads, purchase and in-app activity signals, and diagnostic data. They use it to show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. The ad networks may link this data to the identifiers they assign to your device. Each handles it under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

On iPhone and iPad the app does not ask to track you (no App Tracking Transparency prompt), so the advertising identifier is not available to ads there. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. You can report an inappropriate ad from Profile, which opens an email to us.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To learn whether readers enjoy the app and to fix problems, Pagetease uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC).

  • Usage statistics record app events such as opening the app, starting or finishing a story, reaching a quarter of a story, ads shown or failed, app performance, and purchase steps (no payment details). They carry an app installation identifier, device and app information, and the approximate location Google derives from the IP address. They never contain story titles, story identifiers, genres, tropes or chapter counts. Analytics runs without advertising identifiers.
  • Crash reports contain the error, the state of the app when it crashed, device and operating system information and a Crashlytics installation identifier. Story titles and identifiers are removed from error messages before a report is sent.

Analytics event data is kept for at most 14 months and crash reports for about 90 days. Google processes this data on our behalf; see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Your consent and choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message at launch. Ads, usage statistics and crash reports run only after you agree; until then nothing is sent to analytics or crash reporting. You can change your choice at any time in Profile > Privacy options.

Elsewhere, usage statistics and crash reports are on by default. You can turn off Share anonymous usage statistics and Send crash reports separately in Profile, and they stop at once.

US state privacy laws: aviisr games does not sell your personal information for money. Some state laws treat giving device identifiers to ad networks as "sharing" for targeted advertising. To opt out, delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings, turn on your device's ad tracking limits, or email us and we will help.

Purchases

The Founder's Pass is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app checks the purchase on your device to turn off ads, and you can restore it in Profile.

Age check

Pagetease is for readers aged 18 and over. Where your device offers it (Apple's Declared Age Range on iOS 26 and later, Google Play Age Signals on Android), the app asks for your age range at launch; on other devices it asks you to confirm you are 18 or older. The answer is used only on your device to decide whether the app can open. An adult result is remembered on the device for 7 days. The age signal is never stored by us or sent to advertising, analytics or crash reporting. If the signal shows you are under 18, the app does not open and makes no ad or analytics requests.

App settings file

At launch the app downloads a small signed settings file from aviisrgames.com (hosted by GitHub) that switches ads on or off. Like any website, the host receives your IP address and basic request information; the request contains no identifier and nothing about your reading.

App stores

Apple and Google deliver the app and give us aggregate install, crash and performance statistics, which they handle under their own privacy policies. If you join a Google Play test, Google shares your tester status with us, and we see the email address of testers we invited ourselves.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed or when you ask.

Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Ads, usage statistics and crash reports rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Answering your messages relies on our legitimate interest in providing support. Google and Unity may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own transfer safeguards.

Children

Pagetease is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Your rights

aviisr games has no account or profile about you. Usage statistics and crash reports are tied only to a random installation identifier; we cannot match them to you. To stop collection, turn the switches off; uninstalling also removes the identifier. Stored data expires as described above. For data held by Google or Unity for advertising, use your device's ad settings or their tools linked above. For any privacy request, email aviisr@aviisrgames.com.

Changes

If a later version uses data differently, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
