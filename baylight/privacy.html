Privacy Policy

Baylight: Sort & Ship (Android and iOS) · Effective October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information the Baylight app ("the game", "we") uses and why. Questions: aviisr@gmail.com

Information the game keeps on your device

There are no accounts. Your progress, coins, depot, settings and purchases are saved on your device, and the game works offline. On iPhone and iPad, a copy of your progress is kept in your iCloud account (Apple's key-value storage) so it follows your Apple ID to a new device; we cannot read it. On Android, Google's device backup may restore it on a new phone if you use Android backup. Deleting the game deletes the copy on your device.

Game analytics

To understand how levels are played and to improve them, the game sends gameplay events to Unity Analytics, provided by Unity Technologies. The events are: a level starts, is completed (with stars, jams and time taken), or is restarted; an undo is used (free or after a video); a rewarded video is completed or granted without an ad; a banner is shown; a full-screen ad is shown or skipped after Next; the depot till is collected; a depot object is earned; an offer is shown; a purchase is made. Unity also records standard usage data such as when the game is opened, with device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and a country derived from your IP address. The events carry no name, email or contact details.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Share usage data. When it is off, the game stops sending events, and it asks Unity to delete the data it holds for your device.

Europe, the UK and Switzerland: on devices in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game decides from your mobile network, SIM or device region whether to send analytics and install measurement. If it cannot tell, it sends nothing.

Install measurement

The game reports one "app activation" to Meta (Facebook) each time it starts, on Android and iOS, so Meta can count installs that came from our advertising. No gameplay or purchase events are sent to Meta. On Android, Meta receives your advertising identifier and IP address with that event. On iOS, Meta receives your advertising identifier only if you allowed tracking when iOS asked; otherwise Apple's privacy-preserving attribution is used. Not sent when "Share usage data" is off, or in Europe, the UK and Switzerland.

Advertising

After you clear Bay 5, the game may show a small banner at the top of the screen and, after some completed bays, a full-screen ad when you tap Next. You can also tap a button such as "Double to 28 (ad)" to watch a short video for a bonus. No Ads or Depot Pass in the shop removes the banner and the full-screen ads; videos stay optional. Ads never play in the middle of a sort.

The ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, which works with advertising networks (Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, InMobi, Mintegral, Moloco, Pangle, Chartboost, Bigo). To show and measure ads, LevelPlay and these networks receive your advertising identifier, device and app information, IP address (and a country derived from it) and ad interactions. Each network's use is governed by its own privacy policy.

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: before any ad loads, Google's consent tool (a certified IAB TCF consent platform) asks for your choices. You can change them any time in Settings > Privacy choices. Without consent, ads are not personalized.
  • Elsewhere: you can turn off personalized ads in Settings > Personalized ads. This also tells the networks not to "sell" or "share" your data under US state privacy laws.
  • iOS: the game asks for permission to track (Apple's App Tracking Transparency) once, after level 5. Saying no changes nothing in the game.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Purchases

Purchases (First Shift pack, Depot Pass, Shift Pass, No Ads, coin packs) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never receive your payment details. Purchases are checked and restored through the store itself (Settings > Restore purchases); no other purchase service receives them. Outside Europe, the UK and Switzerland, the game also sends Unity Analytics a "purchase" event with the product name (covered by the analytics opt-out).

Remote settings

The game downloads a small settings file from our website (for example, whether a Shift Pass season is running, whether ads are on, and whether this version of the app must be updated). The request contains no information about you beyond what any web request includes (your IP address).

Children

Baylight is a general-audience puzzle game for players 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off usage data in Settings, change ad choices in Settings (Privacy choices or Personalized ads), limit tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings, and delete all data on your device by deleting the game. To ask for access to, or deletion of, data we hold, email aviisr@gmail.com. If you are in the EU, the UK, California or another region with privacy laws, you have rights under those laws, and we will honor them on request.

Changes

If the game's use of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
