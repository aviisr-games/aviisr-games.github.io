This website

  • No accounts, no forms. The site loads no third-party fonts or cross-site trackers. Apart from the advertising described below, which runs only on 13 specific guide pages, the website sets no cookies.
  • Visit counting. Selected studio, game and guide pages, including the Ninely Facebook-group redirect pages under /go/nfb-*, include an aggregate counter (GoatCounter) that adds up totals only. The group redirect pages count visits to each redirect path, not Facebook group joins. They send no cookies or personal identifiers to GoatCounter. After the redirect, Facebook processes the visit under its own privacy policy. The counter records page paths, referring origin, browser/OS family (from headers without saving raw headers), Google Play outbound clicks, home game-card clicks, QR/YouTube entry tags, first browser move/hint/completion events, language switcher picks, and contact email clicks. Nothing links events together or identifies visitors. It never loads on any Mila page, support page or privacy page, ignores visitors with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, and excludes Mila links on shared pages.
  • Advertising on 13 reviewed guide pages. Only the 12 Sudoku strategy guides (/ninely/strategy/how-to-solve-sudoku/, glossary/, and the 10 technique guides) and the Middle East 2030 strategy guide (/middle-east-2030/guide/) load Google AdSense and Google Funding Choices. Where loaded and permitted, Google and its partners may use cookies, beacons or IP addresses to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses partner-site data, manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com, or opt out via aboutads.info. Ads are never loaded on home, game hubs, playables, privacy or support pages, or any Mila page.
  • Your choice in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and Global Privacy Control. Visitors with Global Privacy Control enabled never load ad code. In consent-required regions, Google's TCF consent dialog asks before personalized/cookie-based ads load, and an on-page Privacy settings link lets you revisit or withdraw consent at any time.
  • Language choice. Your selected display language is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and never leaves your device.
  • Hosting. Hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard edge/server access logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Israeli Privacy Protection Law (Amendment 13)

In accordance with Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (as amended by Amendment 13, effective August 14, 2025):

  • Database controller & contact: aviisr games (Avi Israeli Rozin, Israel), aviisr@aviisrgames.com.
  • Voluntary provision & consequences: You are not legally required to provide personal data to browse this site. All pages and browser games work without an account or forms. If you decline ad cookies or enable Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control, you can still use the entire site; optional features such as personalised ads on guide pages or email support replies require the relevant data.
  • Third-party recipients & cross-border transfers: Technical request data or advertising data on guide pages may be processed by our infrastructure and service providers — GitHub Pages (hosting), GoatCounter (cookieless aggregate visit totals), Google (AdSense on allowlisted guide pages and Gmail for support emails you send us) — including servers in the United States and the European Union.
  • Your statutory rights: Under Section 13 (right to inspect personal data) and Section 14 (right to request correction or deletion of inaccurate, incomplete, unclear, or outdated personal data) of the Protection of Privacy Law, you may submit a request at any time to aviisr@aviisrgames.com.
