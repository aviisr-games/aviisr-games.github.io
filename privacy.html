Privacy Policy

Casino Games (iOS and Android) · Effective October 4, 2026

This policy explains what information the Casino Games app ("the game", "we") uses and why. Questions: aviisr@gmail.com

Information the game keeps on your device

There are no accounts. Your virtual chips, settings, game history and training statistics are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. Deleting the game deletes them.

Support messages

If you contact us (by email or from Settings > Support), we receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to include. The in-game Support button pre-fills basic device details (game version, phone model, operating system, language and screen size); you can see and edit them before sending. We use this only to answer you and fix problems, and delete it when it is no longer needed or when you ask.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads, provided by Unity Technologies and its affiliates. To show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, these services may collect: your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone and iPad only if you allow tracking), an app-specific device identifier, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and how you interact with ads.

Ads are personalized only if you agree. On first launch the game asks whether you want personalized ads; on iPhone and iPad the system tracking prompt appears only if you said yes. If you say no, you see the same number of ads, just not personalized ones.

Unity's privacy policy for game players explains what it collects and how to exercise your rights: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Your choices

  • In the game: Settings > Personalized ads turns personalization on or off at any time.
  • iPhone/iPad: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads (reset or delete your advertising ID).
  • In many ads: tap the privacy icon to see why you were shown the ad and opt out.
  • Report an inappropriate ad: Settings > Support > Report an ad.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information and to withdraw consent. We only hold support emails; write to us and we will reply within 30 days. For data collected by the ad services, follow Unity's policy linked above.

Children

The game contains simulated gambling and is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. No real money is involved: chips cannot be bought, cashed out or transferred.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new effective date.
