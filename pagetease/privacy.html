Privacy Policy

Pagetease (Android closed beta) · Effective November 26, 2026

Pagetease is an illustrated romance reading app for adults, published by aviisr games. This policy explains what the app does with information during the Google Play closed beta. Questions: aviisr@aviisrgames.com

What we collect

Nothing. The closed beta build does not send personal data, account details, location, device identifiers or your reading activity to aviisr games or to anyone else. There are no accounts.

What stays on your device

Your reading progress, saved stories, and text and theme settings are stored only on your device. You can clear them at any time in Profile > Delete local data, or by uninstalling the app.

Ads, analytics and purchases

The closed beta contains no ads, no advertising or analytics SDKs, and no purchases. Every chapter of the bundled stories is free to read.

Google Play

Google Play delivers the app and gives us aggregate install, crash and performance statistics (Google Play Console). Google handles that data under its own privacy policy. If you join the closed test, Google shares your tester status with us, and we see the email address of testers we invited ourselves.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed or when you ask.

Age

Pagetease is for readers aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Your choices and rights

Because the beta collects nothing, there is no personal data for us to access or delete. You can remove everything the app stores by deleting local data or uninstalling it. For any privacy request, email aviisr@aviisrgames.com.

Changes

If a later version uses data differently (for example ads or purchases), we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
