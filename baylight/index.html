Baylight: Sort & Ship

Support

Baylight is a calm one-thumb sorting puzzle. Tap a bay, stack three of a kind, and ship the truck.

Contact

Email aviisr@aviisrgames.com. Tell us your device model, your operating system version and what happened. We reply by email.

Common questions

A bay jammed. Nothing is lost. Undo is free twice at first, then once after ads start (twice with Depot Pass), or restart the level.

Ads. After Bay 5: a small banner at the top, an occasional full-screen ad after Next, and optional videos for bonuses. Depot Pass in the shop removes ads.

I want to stop the usage data. Turn off Settings > Share usage data.

I want my data deleted. Delete the game to remove everything on your device. For data held by Unity, email us and we will help.
