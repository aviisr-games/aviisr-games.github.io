Privacy Policy

Beamwise: Light Puzzle (iOS and Android) · Effective November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the Beamwise app ("the game", "we") uses and why. The game is made by aviisr games (Avi Israeli Rozin, Israel). Questions: aviisr@aviisrgames.com

Information the game keeps on your device

There are no accounts, no names and no emails, and the game works offline. Your progress and settings are stored on your device. We do not run our own servers for the game; the only service the game contacts for its own settings is Unity, described below under Game settings and anonymous sign-in. Deleting the game deletes the information on your device, including the random player ID described below.

Game analytics

To understand which levels are too hard or too easy and to fix problems, the game can use Unity Analytics (Unity Gaming Services). Analytics are off by default and only start if you turn on Menu > Shop & Settings > Share anonymous gameplay stats. Turning it off again stops collection. When it is on, it records gameplay events such as starting, finishing or retrying a level, showing the How to Play screen, watching an optional rewarded ad, and starting or completing a purchase (the product name only). Unity also receives a random installation ID created by the game, device and app information (such as model, operating system and app version), the country derived from your IP address, and diagnostic information. This data is not linked to your name or contact details and is not used for advertising or tracking. Unity processes it for us under its privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. You can ask us to delete it by emailing us.

Game settings and anonymous sign-in (Unity)

So we can adjust game balance without releasing an update (for example how often between-level ads appear, Glow amounts and when hints unlock), the game downloads its settings from Unity Remote Config (Unity Gaming Services). To do this, the game uses Unity Authentication to sign in anonymously: Unity creates a random player ID. There is no name, email address or account, and Beamwise does not use Unity accounts and never asks for your email.

This happens after Google's consent tool has finished. Outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, where consent is not required, the game does this at start-up even when Share anonymous gameplay stats is off. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, it happens only if you have turned on gameplay stats. If the settings cannot be downloaded, or you are offline, the game uses the settings built into the app.

For this, Unity may receive the random installation and player IDs, device and app information (such as model, operating system and app version), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, and diagnostic and crash information. Unity's SDK privacy manifests also list email address, other contact information and customer support data, which relate to Unity account features that Beamwise does not use; we do not send any of these to Unity. This data is not linked to your name or contact details and is not used for advertising or tracking. Unity processes it for us under its privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. You can ask us to delete it by emailing us, and deleting the game removes the player ID from your device.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob mediation. AdMob works with these ad partners, and each one is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Liftoff Monetize (Vungle): liftoff.ai/privacy-policy
  • Moloco Inc. (Moloco Ads), an advertising network that may receive your advertising identifier, IP address and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads. Privacy: moloco.com/terms-and-policies/privacy-portal
  • Index Exchange (bids through Google): indexexchange.com/privacy

The game shows two kinds of ads: optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch on the win screen for in-game Glow (Glow is earned by playing and is never sold), and, from your second session on, at most one full-screen ad between levels after you tap NEXT on a win screen (at most one per three wins, at least five minutes apart and at most three per day). The one-time purchase Remove Auto Ads removes the between-level ads. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages. If you see an inappropriate ad, use Menu > Shop & Settings > Report an ad or email us.

To show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, the ad SDKs and partners may collect: your device's advertising identifier (the advertising ID on Android, and the IDFA on iPhone and iPad only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information (such as model, operating system, language and app version), how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information. We do not receive this data about you; it goes to the ad providers.

Consent and your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and you can change your choices at any time in Menu > Shop & Settings > Privacy Choices. In US states with privacy laws, Google's consent tool lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for personalized ads. On iPhone and iPad, the game also shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, the game works exactly the same, and ads are shown without the advertising identifier. You can also reset or turn off your advertising identifier in your device settings (on iOS, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

Purchases

In-app purchases (Remove Auto Ads, the Night Sky Pack, a cosmetic theme, and the Supporter Bundle, which includes both) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The game receives a purchase confirmation from the store so it can unlock what you bought. Purchases are restored through the store, or with Restore Purchases in the menu.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to include. We use it only to answer you and fix problems, and we delete it when it is no longer needed or when you ask. Crash and install statistics are provided to us by Apple (App Store Connect) and Google (Google Play Console) in aggregate form.

Children

Beamwise is a general-audience puzzle game for players 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Retention

Progress and settings stay on your device until you delete the game. We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you. Ad providers keep data under their own retention rules, described in the policies linked above.

Your choices and rights

You can turn gameplay analytics on or off (the anonymous settings download described above is part of how the game works and is not affected by this switch outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), change your ad consent choices in Settings, decline tracking on iPhone and iPad, reset or turn off your advertising identifier in your device settings, and delete all data on your device, including the random player ID, by deleting the game, and ask us to delete the data Unity holds (analytics and game settings) by email. People in the EU, the UK and Switzerland (GDPR), California (CCPA/CPRA), Israel and other regions have rights under their local privacy laws, such as access, correction, deletion and objecting to personalized advertising. Because we hold no account data about you, most requests about ad data must go to the ad provider concerned, using the policies above. To ask us about anything we hold, or to exercise a right, email aviisr@aviisrgames.com, and we will honor it.

Changes

If the game's use of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
