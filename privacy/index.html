This website

  • No cookies, no accounts, no forms. The site loads no third-party fonts, ads or trackers.
  • Visit counting. Some pages include a counter (GoatCounter) that only adds up totals. It counts:
    • visits to each page, with the referring website;
    • the browser and operating system name (for example Chrome on Android), worked out from the browser header, which itself is not stored;
    • clicks on a Google Play button, and on which page;
    • which link on a home page game card was clicked (play in the browser, or the about page);
    • a page opened on a phone from the QR code shown on a computer;
    • a page opened from a link on our YouTube channel (the link carries a short tag, so we can tell it came from the channel);
    • the first move or hint in a browser game, and a finished Ninely or Baylight puzzle or Middle East 2030 briefing (for the briefing, which of its eight possible decision paths, made of three A or B choices, was taken);
    • a language picked from this site's language menu (the language code only);
    • clicks on the contact email link, and on which page.
    Nothing links one count to another or to a person: there are no cookies, no personal identifiers and no sessions, and the counter does not track visitors across pages or visits. Clicks on links to Mila pages are not counted. It does not record country, region, your browser's language setting or screen size. It deletes the data after 400 days. It does not count visitors who turn on Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
  • Never on Mila pages. The counter never runs on the Mila pages, which are for parents of children, or on privacy and support pages.
  • Language choice. Your language choice is saved in your browser (local storage) so the next page opens in the same language. The saved setting never leaves your device; only the language picked from the menu is counted, as a total (see Visit counting).
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.
