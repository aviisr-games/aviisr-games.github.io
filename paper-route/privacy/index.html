Privacy Policy: Morning Paper Route

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how "Morning Paper Route" (the "Game"), developed and published by Aviisr ("we", "us", or "our"), handles your information. Morning Paper Route has no user accounts, no sign-in requirements, no chat functionality, and operates without custom remote game servers.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Game stores only gameplay progress and local preferences directly on your device storage (such as in local JSON save files or player preferences). This data is never sent to our own servers:

  • Sound, music, and haptic feedback preferences;
  • Gameplay progress, unlocked routes (Days 1 to 7), and best star ratings;
  • Earned in-game route coins and unlocked bike and rider cosmetics;
  • Input sensitivity, handedness, and accessibility settings (such as Reduced Motion);
  • Advertising privacy consent preferences.

Uninstalling the Game or clearing application data removes this information from your device.

2. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

Morning Paper Route is supported by advertisements: optional rewarded videos (a revive, extra newspapers or bonus coins, always chosen by the player) and, occasionally, a short full-screen ad between game days. Ads never appear during a ride, and the Game shows no banner ads.

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play or the App Store; we do not receive or store your payment details.

Advertisements are mediated and delivered by:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation platform by Unity Technologies)
  • Unity Ads (ad network by Unity Technologies)

To serve, measure, and protect against fraudulent ad views, these advertising providers may automatically collect certain device telemetry:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFV/IDFA where permitted);
  • General coarse geographic location inferred from IP address;
  • Device technical information (device manufacturer, model, operating system version, screen resolution, locale);
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, completion status, and ad clicks);
  • Basic diagnostic and performance metrics.

Before any ad requests are made, players can manage their privacy preferences in the Game. When personalized advertising is declined, the Game signals our mediation partners to serve non-personalized ads.

For details on how Unity Technologies processes information, please review their privacy policies:

  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Optional gameplay analytics

If you turn on "Share gameplay statistics" (off by default; also offered on the first-launch privacy screen), the Game sends limited gameplay events to Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies). Examples include day start/end, tutorial completion, ad offers, and in-app purchase entitlements. This can include device or other identifiers and app interaction data. It is optional, not used to personalize ads, and opting out stops collection and requests deletion of that player's collected analytics data.

Support ID for ad quality

The Game creates a random per-install support ID (not your name or email) and may pass it to LevelPlay Ad Quality so we can look up which ad a player saw when you report a problem. It is stored only on your device with your save data.

Local reminders

If you allow notifications, the Game can schedule local reminders on your device (for example a morning route reminder). These stay on the device; we do not operate a push server for them.

Feedback and ad reports

Send feedback opens an in-game form that posts to a Google Form we operate. You can include a message and an optional email address. The form also receives device and build details (version, model, OS, language) so we can reproduce problems. We use feedback only to answer you and improve the Game. Google processes the form submission under its own terms.

Report an ad opens your email app with a pre-filled message to us (including a short support ID when available). Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email.

3. In-App Purchases and Payments

Morning Paper Route offers optional one-time, non-consumable purchases processed by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details, only whether each purchase is active on your store account:

  • Remove Ads removes the short ads between game days. Optional rewarded videos (revive, extra papers, double coins) stay available.
  • Town packs unlock additional neighborhood routes. Bike and rider cosmetics are still earned through gameplay and in-game coins.

4. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, or physical addresses to play. If you include an email in feedback or email us directly, we receive that address and message only to reply.
  • We do not request access to camera, microphone, or photo library.
  • We do not track precise GPS location.
  • We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Children and Age Rating

Morning Paper Route is designed as a general-audience mobile arcade game (ages 13 and older). The Game is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe that an ad provider has collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately and we will coordinate deletion with the provider.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have legal rights to access, delete, or limit the processing and sharing of your personal data for targeted advertising. Because we do not operate user accounts or retain user data on remote servers, you can exercise these rights directly by:

  • Adjusting your privacy, ad consent, and "Share gameplay statistics" choices inside the in-game Settings menu;
  • Resetting or deleting your Advertising ID via your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking);
  • Deleting the application to clear all local device storage;
  • Contacting us at the email address below for any privacy inquiries.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here with an updated revision date.
