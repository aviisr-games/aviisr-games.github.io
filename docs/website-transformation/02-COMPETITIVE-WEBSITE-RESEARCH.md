# 02 - Competitive website research

Research date: 2026-11-12. Method: live pages fetched as text by a research agent, so layout and motion were read from markup, not watched. Statements are **[O]** observed in the fetched page or **[I]** inferred. No screenshots of other studios were captured.

Not retrieved: puzzmo.com, nytimes.com/crosswords, snowman.is, duolingo.com/abc, popcore.com. monumentvalleygame.com was partial (it needs JavaScript).

## 2. Patterns that recur

1. Game art carries the page. Every studio that shows its games leads with key art or screenshots, never with icons alone. (Supercell, ustwo, Halfbrick, Playstack, Rovio, Noodlecake) [O]
2. One block or tile per game, each with a one-line hook. (ustwo, Zachtronics, Noodlecake) [O]
3. Official store badges, unmodified, repeated per game on small catalogues and in the footer on large ones. [O]
4. Puzzle products let the visitor play before anything else. (sudoku.com) [O]
5. Kids products talk to the parent and list concrete privacy facts. (Khan Academy Kids) [O]
6. No site in the sample showed device detection or a QR code. The existing Aviisr QR handoff is already ahead here. [I]

## 3. Trends to avoid

1. News or stories above the games (ustwo, Playstack, Supercell). It pushes products below the fold. [O]
2. A video hero with no text or fallback (Playstack). Heavy, and it says nothing if the video fails. [O]
3. Mega-menus and long headers (Kwalee, sudoku.com). Five products need at most four navigation items. [O]
4. Corporate sections a one-person studio cannot fill: careers, investments, sustainability. [O]
5. Unnamed, text-only product lists (Voodoo). [O]

## 4. What transfers, in priority order

1. Show real game art above the fold. [J]
2. One full-width block per game, each in the game's own colours, with one line of copy, a play action and the store badge. [J]
3. For Ninely, shorten the path to a live board. [J]
4. For Mila, a parent trust block built from facts already published on the Mila pages. [J]
5. Genre tag on each game block. [J]

## 5. Limits of this research

- Text capture cannot confirm autoplay, motion quality or real mobile layout on the reference sites. [E]
- No conversion data from any of these studios is public, so every "works well" above is a design judgment, not a measured result. [E]
- Large studios optimise for brand and recruiting, not installs from the site. Their choices are weak evidence for a studio with tens of installs. [J]
