Middle East 2030: Politics Sim

A political strategy game for Android. A work of fiction set in an invented 2030: no event, headline or leader in it is real.

2030. Can you hold the region for one term?

Pick one of eight capitals, take the chair, and govern one month at a time through a term of six to eight months. Each month one decision is enough (a second is optional), then one tap prints the morning paper and tomorrow's front page shows what your choices did.

New players get a short guided first term that ends at their first paper, and every finished term builds a career: Rising Leader, Negotiator, Strategist, Veteran, Statesman.
