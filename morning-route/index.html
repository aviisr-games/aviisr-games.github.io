Morning Route

Paper Rush - Mobile Arcade Cycling Game for iOS and Android.

About the Game

Deliver the morning news on your bicycle! Dodge traffic, avoid barking dogs, jump curbs, and toss papers accurately onto subscriber doorsteps before your shift ends.

  • Fast-paced 60 FPS arcade delivery gameplay
  • 7 distinct neighborhood delivery routes with star ratings
  • Unlockable rider outfits and bicycle styles earned strictly through gameplay coins
  • Portrait one-handed or two-handed controls with customizable sensitivity and handedness
