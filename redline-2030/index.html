Middle East 2030

Middle East 2030 is a political strategy game set in a fictional 2030. Lead a nation through one short term, one month at a time. Every choice is tomorrow's front page.

Get it on Google Play Not on iPhone yet

What you do

  • Choose one of eight capitals, each with its own neighbors, pressures and hard choices.
  • Make two decisions a month. Every option shows its price in one plain sentence before you commit.
  • Read the morning paper. Friends and rivals react, and every shift comes with a reason you can see.
  • Keep your coalition, your economy and your public with you to the end of the term.
  • Win re-election for up to three terms.

Why it is different

  • Sessions of a few minutes. A whole term fits on a commute.
  • No side is the villain. Every capital is playable and every government has its reasons.
  • One daily world for everyone (the Morning Edition), so you can compare front pages with friends.
  • Five kinds of ending, from a long quiet to a collapse nobody planned.

A work of fiction

Every person, newspaper and armed group in the game is invented. The game shows the cost of decisions, never graphic violence, and contains no real leaders.
