Support

Questions

Are there ads? Optional video ads can revive you, refill your papers or double your coins, and a short ad can play between days, never during a ride.

How do I remove ads? Buy "Remove ads" once in Settings or the Cosmetics shop. It turns off the ad between days. The optional reward videos (revive, extra papers, double coins) stay available whenever you want them.

I reinstalled or changed phones. Open Settings and tap "Restore purchases" (signed in to the same Google Play or App Store account).

How do I turn off personalized ads? Settings > Ad privacy choices > Non-personalized ads. On iPhone you can also turn off tracking in the system Settings.

Does the game need the internet? No, you can play offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.

I saw an inappropriate ad. Use "Report an ad" in Settings, or email us.
