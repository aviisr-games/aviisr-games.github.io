About aviisr games

An independent studio making small mobile games, and writing about how they work.

Who we are

aviisr games is a one-person studio run by Avi Israeli. We publish all five titles on Google Play (IL, US), with Middle East 2030, Baylight (IL, US) and Mila (on the App Store in Israel) also available on the App Store: Ninely (a sudoku game that explains every move), Middle East 2030 (a political strategy simulation set in an invented 2030), Baylight (a one-thumb truck-sorting puzzle), Morning Paper Route (a bike arcade game) and Mila (an English-learning app for children in Hebrew-speaking families).

The games are small on purpose. Each one tries to do one thing well, to start fast and to respect the player's time and privacy. This website follows the same rule: static pages, no accounts, no sign-up forms, and an aggregate visit counter that excludes children's and policy pages.

What you will find here

  • Game pages: each public title has its own hub with verified store links, real screenshots and, where available, a browser version you can try without installing anything.
  • Sudoku guides: twelve step-by-step guides to human-logic solving techniques, from naked singles to Swordfish and Y-Wing, with solver-checked example boards.
  • Strategy guide: a companion dossier for Middle East 2030 covering the eight playable capitals, monthly choices and briefing outcomes.
  • Devlog notes: short notes on how we generate puzzles, verify browser solvers and design game rules.
  • Printable worksheets: free Hebrew and English flashcards and practice sheets for Mila learners, with matched answer keys.

Advertising and privacy

Our mobile games on Google Play (and Middle East 2030 and Baylight on the App Store) are free and may show ads or offer optional purchases, as described in each game's privacy policy; Mila is available on Google Play and the App Store in Israel with no ads and a one-time unlock. On this website, only the 12 Sudoku strategy guide pages and the Middle East 2030 strategy guide load advertising via Google AdSense (when permitted by consent and Global Privacy Control). Game hubs, browser playables, notes, About, support pages, privacy pages and every Mila page are ad-free.
