Privacy Policy: Middle East 2030

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains what information the game "Middle East 2030" (the "Game"), published by aviisr-games ("we"), uses and why. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no server of its own.

1. What stays on your device

The Game saves the following only on your phone or tablet, and never sends it to us:

  • your language and sound settings,
  • your progress (the run in progress and how many runs you finished),
  • your ad privacy choice,
  • whether you bought "Remove ads".

Deleting the Game deletes this information.

2. Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads, unless you buy "Remove ads". Ads are delivered by:

  • Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource), the ad mediation service, and
  • Unity Ads, an ad network.

Both are provided by Unity Technologies. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, these services may collect from your device:

  • device and advertising identifiers (for example Apple's IDFA, Google's Advertising ID, or IDFV),
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, network type),
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and app interactions,
  • diagnostic and performance data.

Before any ad is requested, the Game asks you once whether ads may use data to be more relevant. You can change this at any time under "Ad privacy" on the title screen.

  • If you choose limited ads, the Game tells the ad services that you did not consent to personalized advertising.
  • On iPhone and iPad, Apple's tracking prompt appears only if you chose personalized ads, and your answer to Apple's prompt always wins.

These services process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity (including LevelPlay and Unity Ads): https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • ironSource / LevelPlay: https://developers.is.com/ironsource-mobile/air/ironsource-mobile-privacy-policy/

You can also reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings.

3. Purchases

"Remove ads" is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They process the payment. We never see your payment details. The Game only checks that you own the purchase.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • The Game's own code sends no analytics to any server.

5. Sharing a front page

If you tap "Share", the Game creates a picture of your final front page and opens your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

6. Children

The Game is a political strategy game made for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). The ad services are told that the Game is not child-directed. If you believe a child has used the Game and data was collected, contact us and we will help you request deletion from the ad services.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.

  • Because we hold no personal data ourselves, the fastest way to exercise these rights over advertising data is the "Ad privacy" setting in the Game, your device's advertising settings, and the ad services' policies linked above.
  • You can also write to us and we will pass your request on.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and the version inside the Game.
