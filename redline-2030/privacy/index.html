Privacy Policy: Middle East 2030

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information the game "Middle East 2030" (the "Game"), published by aviisr-games ("we"), uses and why. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no server of its own.

1. What stays on your device

The Game saves the following only on your phone or tablet, and never sends it to us:

  • your settings (language, text size, music, sound effects, alarm sounds, vibration, animations, reminders and the newspaper style),
  • your progress (the run in progress, how many runs you finished, and your Morning Edition results and streak),
  • your ad privacy choice,
  • which purchases you own (a copy, so the Game works offline).

Deleting the Game deletes this information.

What the Game itself sends

  • Game analytics (from version 1.6): the Game sends anonymous usage events to Unity Gaming Services Analytics (for example: a term started, a month printed, a term ended and how, a code entered, a purchase completed), with a random install identifier created by Unity. No name, email, contacts or free text. They are sent only if you chose "Allow relevant ads", or if your region does not require consent; choosing "Limit ad data" in "Ad privacy" stops them. Unity's policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Leaderboards (iPhone): when a term ends, or when you open the leaderboard, the Game asks Apple's Game Center to sign you in and sends your legacy score to Game Center. This is Apple's service, under Apple's privacy policy; if you are not signed in to Game Center, nothing is sent.
  • Reminders: if you allow notifications, the Game schedules up to three reminders on your device when you leave. They are local notifications: nothing is sent to a server, and opening the Game cancels them. You can turn them off on the title screen.

2. Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads, unless you buy "Remove ads". Ads are delivered by:

  • Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource), the ad mediation service, and
  • these ad networks: Unity Ads, Google AdMob, InMobi, Moloco, Chartboost, Pangle, BIGO Ads, Meta Audience Network, AppLovin and Mintegral.

LevelPlay and Unity Ads are provided by Unity Technologies; Google AdMob by Google; InMobi by InMobi; Moloco by Moloco; Chartboost by Chartboost (part of LoopMe); Pangle by Bytedance; BIGO Ads by BIGO Technology; Meta Audience Network by Meta Platforms; AppLovin by AppLovin; and Mintegral by Mintegral. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, these services may collect from your device:

  • device and advertising identifiers (for example Apple's IDFA, Google's Advertising ID, or IDFV),
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, network type),
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and app interactions,
  • diagnostic and performance data.

Before any ad is requested, the Game asks you once whether ads may use data to be more relevant. You can change this at any time under "Ad privacy" on the title screen.

  • If you choose limited ads, the Game tells the ad services that you did not consent to personalized advertising.
  • On iPhone and iPad, Apple's tracking prompt appears only if you chose personalized ads, and your answer to Apple's prompt always wins.

These services process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity (including LevelPlay and Unity Ads): https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • ironSource / LevelPlay: https://developers.is.com/ironsource-mobile/air/ironsource-mobile-privacy-policy/
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy (how Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
  • InMobi: https://www.inmobi.com/privacy-policy
  • Moloco: https://www.moloco.com/privacy-policy
  • Pangle: https://www.pangleglobal.com/privacy/enduser-en
  • Bigo Ads: https://www.bigoads.com/privacy

  • AppLovin: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/
  • Mintegral: https://www.mintegral.com/en/privacy

You can also reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings.

3. Purchases

"Remove ads", the Cabinet Edition, scenario packs and newspaper themes are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They process the payment. We never see your payment details. The Game only checks that you own the purchase.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • "Send feedback" opens your own email app with a draft to us, including the app version, your device model and system, the language and your current run's code. You see the draft and choose whether to send it; we use it only to answer and to fix problems.

5. Sharing a front page

If you tap "Share", the Game creates a picture of your final front page and opens your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

6. Children

The Game is a political strategy game made for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). The ad services are told that the Game is not child-directed. If you believe a child has used the Game and data was collected, contact us and we will help you request deletion from the ad services. Where the store's age check applies to your account (for example, under some US state laws or in Brazil), the Game asks Apple (iPhone, from version 2.3) or Google Play (Android, from version 2.3.1) for your age range. It never learns your birth date, only whether you are under 13, 13 to 17, or an adult, and keeps that answer on your device. If you are under 18, or on iPhone choose not to share, the Game turns off relevant ads and game analytics whatever you chose in "Ad privacy"; under 13 the ad services are also told the Game is being used by a child.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.

  • Because we hold no personal data ourselves, the fastest way to exercise these rights over advertising data is the "Ad privacy" setting in the Game, your device's advertising settings, and the ad services' policies linked above.
  • You can also write to us and we will pass your request on.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and the version inside the Game.
