Privacy Policy

Pagetease (Android and iOS) · Effective November 26, 2026 (updated November 26, 2026)

Pagetease is an illustrated romance reading app for adults, published by aviisr games. This policy explains what the app does with information. Questions: aviisr@aviisrgames.com

What we collect

aviisr games does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, and your reading activity never leaves your device. The only data that leaves the app is what the advertising service below needs when ads are shown.

What stays on your device

Your reading progress, saved stories, and text and theme settings are stored only on your device. You can clear them at any time in Profile > Delete local data, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising

Pagetease can show ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC): a full-screen ad at a chapter break after you tap Continue, and an optional ad you choose to watch for ad-free pages. During the current beta, ads are switched off. When ads are on, Google AdMob and its ad partners may collect, to show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud: your device's advertising identifier, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent tool before any ad is requested, and ads are personalized only if you agree. You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. On iPhone and iPad the app does not ask to track you, so ads there are not personalized with your advertising identifier.

Purchases and analytics

There are no purchases and no analytics services in the app today. Every chapter of the bundled stories is free to read.

Google Play

Google Play delivers the app and gives us aggregate install, crash and performance statistics (Google Play Console). Google handles that data under its own privacy policy. If you join the closed test, Google shares your tester status with us, and we see the email address of testers we invited ourselves.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed or when you ask.

Age

Pagetease is for readers aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Your choices and rights

aviisr games holds no personal data about you to access or delete. For data held by Google for advertising, use your device's ad settings or Google's own tools. You can remove everything the app stores by deleting local data or uninstalling it. For any privacy request, email aviisr@aviisrgames.com.

Changes

If a later version uses data differently (for example purchases or analytics), we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
