This Privacy Policy explains how "Morning Paper Route" (the "Game"), developed and published by Aviisr ("we", "us", or "our"), handles your information. Morning Paper Route has no user accounts, no sign-in requirements, no chat functionality, and operates without custom remote game servers.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Game stores only gameplay progress and local preferences directly on your device storage (such as in local JSON save files or player preferences). This data is never sent to our own servers:

  • Sound, music, and haptic feedback preferences;
  • Gameplay progress, unlocked routes (Days 1 to 7), and best star ratings;
  • Earned in-game route coins and unlocked bike and rider cosmetics;
  • Input sensitivity, handedness, and accessibility settings (such as Reduced Motion);
  • Advertising privacy consent preferences;
  • A random support ID, and, while "Share gameplay statistics" is on, the random Firebase analytics IDs your installation has used.

Uninstalling the Game or clearing application data removes this information from your device.

2. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

Morning Paper Route is supported by advertisements: optional rewarded videos (a revive, extra newspapers or bonus coins, always chosen by the player) and, occasionally, a short full-screen ad between game days. No ad interrupts a ride on its own: the only ad that can play during a ride is the optional extra-newspapers video, and only when you tap it. Remove Ads turns off the between-day ads.

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play or the App Store; we do not receive or store your payment details.

From version 1.2.0 the ads are served through Google AdMob mediation, which works with these advertising networks:

  • Google AdMob (ad mediation platform and ad network by Google)
  • Unity Ads (ad network by Unity Technologies)
  • Meta Audience Network (ad network by Meta Platforms)
  • Liftoff Monetize (ad network by Liftoff, formerly Vungle)
  • InMobi (ad network by InMobi)
  • Chartboost (ad network by Chartboost)
  • Moloco Inc. (Moloco Ads) (an advertising network that may receive your advertising identifier, IP address and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads)

Earlier versions use Unity LevelPlay, which works with Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, InMobi, Chartboost, Moloco and Liftoff Monetize. Each network's use is governed by its own privacy policy (links below).

To serve, measure, and protect against fraudulent ad views, these advertising providers may automatically collect certain device telemetry:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFV/IDFA where permitted);
  • General coarse geographic location inferred from IP address;
  • Device technical information (device manufacturer, model, operating system version, screen resolution, locale);
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, completion status, and ad clicks);
  • Basic diagnostic and performance metrics.

Before any ad requests are made, players can manage their privacy preferences in the Game. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the Game first shows Google's consent form (UMP) so you can accept or decline the ad partners listed there. You can reopen those choices from Settings > Ad privacy choices. When personalized advertising is declined, the Game signals our mediation partners to serve non-personalized ads. Where US state privacy laws apply, declining personalized ads also tells Google and our mediation partners not to "sell" or "share" your data (restricted data processing). Where Google shows its US state privacy message, you can opt out there as well and change that choice later from Settings > Ad privacy choices.

On iPhone and iPad, the Game also shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt ("Allow tracking?"). New players are asked a moment after the results of their first ride; returning players are asked when the Game opens. The ad SDK waits for your answer, for up to two minutes; without permission it cannot use your advertising identifier. Your answer to Apple's prompt always wins, and every game feature works the same either way.

These services process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (mediation and ad network): https://policies.google.com/privacy (how Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
  • Unity (Unity Ads, and LevelPlay in earlier versions): https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Meta Audience Network: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Liftoff Monetize: https://liftoff.ai/privacy-policy/
  • InMobi: https://www.inmobi.com/privacy-policy
  • Chartboost (LoopMe): https://legal.loopme.com/privacy-center
  • Moloco Inc. (Moloco Ads, and Moloco in earlier versions): https://www.moloco.com/terms-and-policies/privacy-portal

Leaderboards

The Game has a weekly leaderboard through Game Center (Apple, on iOS) or Google Play Games (Google, on Android). If you are signed in to that service, your weekly score is sent to it and shown with your player name from that service; Apple or Google handle that account under their own privacy policies. If you are not signed in, no score is sent.

Remote configuration

The Game uses Unity Remote Config (Unity Technologies) to switch a small number of finished game features on or off without requiring an app update. Once per launch, after your first-launch privacy choice, it connects with an anonymous Unity installation identifier (and an anonymous Unity sign-in) to retrieve these settings. This configuration request is not used to collect gameplay analytics. One of these settings is a switch that tells the Game whether it may start the Google Firebase services described below; it has the same value for every player and the request sends no player attributes, so it cannot be used to single out or target a player. If the switch cannot be read, the Game does not start those services.

Gameplay statistics (Unity Analytics)

The Game has one switch, "Share gameplay statistics", on the first-launch privacy screen and in Settings > Privacy and data. You can turn it off at any time. On the first-launch screen the switch starts on where Google's consent check says no consent is needed, and off in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland or when the region is unknown. In version 1.2.3, Google Firebase (below) starts only if you have turned the switch on yourself. From version 1.2.4, Google Firebase follows this same switch, with the age rules described under Google Firebase Analytics below.

When it is on, the Game sends limited gameplay events to Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies): for example day start and end, tutorial completion, ad offers and in-app purchase attempts. This can include device or other identifiers and app interaction data. Unity keeps this data for 13 months. It is optional and not used to personalize ads.

Unity Analytics is not used for a player whom the app store has identified as under 13 (today Google Play only), even if the switch is on.

Google Firebase Analytics (Google)

When Firebase is allowed to start (see "When it starts" below), the Game also sends gameplay events to Firebase Analytics, a service of Google LLC and its group, to help us understand how the Game is used and to fix problems.

  • What is sent: gameplay and app events (for example day start, tutorial steps, ad offers, purchase events), a random Firebase app instance ID, app version, device model, operating system and language, and an approximate location (country or city level) that Google derives from your IP address. We do not send your name, email address or location from your device, and we do not give Firebase your advertising ID. Google signals are off, all Google Analytics data sharing settings with Google are off, and we have accepted the Google Ads Data Processing Terms.
  • Ad data combined with Firebase: our Firebase project is linked to our Google AdMob account, so that ad impressions and ad revenue from the Game (which ad was shown, its network and the revenue it earned, not who you are) are combined with the Firebase Analytics events of the same app installation. We use this to see how ads affect play and revenue. It is not used to show you ads or to personalize ads.
  • Purpose: statistics about use of the Game and about how our ads perform (analytics). It is not used to show or personalize ads.
  • Export to BigQuery: Firebase Analytics events are exported daily to a Google BigQuery project (Google Cloud) that we control, so that we can analyze them with our own queries. The export contains the same data as above (events, the random app instance ID, device and app details, approximate location, the combined ad impression and revenue events) and is used only by us, only for analysis. It is stored in the United States and kept for 60 days.
  • When it starts, version 1.2.3: Firebase does not start, and sends nothing, until you have turned on "Share gameplay statistics" yourself on a device where the Game may use it. A switch that was on by regional default, or carried over from an earlier version, does not start Firebase.
  • When it starts, from version 1.2.4: Firebase follows the "Share gameplay statistics" switch in the same way as Unity Analytics. For players aged 18 and over, and for players with no age signal (today this includes all iPhone and iPad players), Firebase starts when the switch is on, including where the switch started on because Google's consent check says no consent is needed in your region. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, or when the region is unknown, the switch starts off and Firebase starts only after you turn it on. For a player whom Google Play has identified as 13 to 17, Firebase starts only if you have turned the switch on yourself; a switch that is on by regional default does not count. Turning the switch off stops Firebase at once, in every version.
  • Remote switch: in every version the Game starts Firebase only if the remote setting described under Remote configuration allows it. We can turn Firebase off for all players with that setting, without an app update.
  • Not for children under 13: the Game is not for children under 13. Firebase is not started for a player whom the app store has identified as under 13 (today this signal exists on Google Play only; Apple does not give the Game an age signal in this version).
  • Where processed: Google may process the data in the United States and other countries where Google operates.
  • How long: event data and user-level data are each kept for 2 months in Google Analytics. Exported BigQuery tables are kept for 60 days.
  • Provider's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

When Firebase is allowed to start (see "When it starts" under Firebase Analytics), the Game also sends crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics so we can find and fix crashes.

  • What is sent: the crash report (stack trace of the code that crashed), app version, device model, operating system version and memory and storage state at the time, and a random installation ID. We do not add your name, email address or advertising ID.
  • Purpose: finding and fixing crashes (app functionality and diagnostics). Not used for advertising.
  • Same conditions as Firebase Analytics, in each version: version 1.2.3 only after you turn the switch on yourself, version 1.2.4 as described under "When it starts, from version 1.2.4", and never started for a player whom the app store has identified as under 13.
  • Export to BigQuery: crash reports are also exported to the same Google BigQuery project that we control, used only by us, only for analysis. They are stored in the United States and kept for 60 days.
  • How long: Crashlytics keeps crash data for 90 days, after which Google starts removing it.
  • Where processed: as for Firebase Analytics.

Firebase installation ID

Firebase creates a random installation ID on your device so that Analytics and Crashlytics can tell installs apart. It is kept until it is deleted, and Google removes it automatically after 270 days without use. It is not your name or your advertising ID.

Retention at a glance

  • Unity Analytics: 13 months.
  • Firebase Analytics events and user-level data: 2 months.
  • BigQuery export (stored in the United States): 60 days.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: 90 days.
  • Firebase installation ID: until it is deleted, or removed by Google after 270 days without use.

What happens when you turn "Share gameplay statistics" off

  • Unity Analytics: collection stops and the Game asks Unity to delete the data collected for your installation.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: collection stops at once and the Game resets Firebase's analytics ID on your device. Data already sent is not deleted by turning the switch off: it expires on its own (2 months for events in Google Analytics, 60 days in BigQuery, 90 days for crash reports). You can ask us to delete it sooner, see Delete Your Data.

Support ID

The Game creates a random support ID for your installation (not your name or email) and stores it on your device with your save data. In Settings > Privacy and data it is shown as one string that you can copy. While "Share gameplay statistics" is on, the string also contains the random Firebase analytics IDs your installation has used, including earlier ones, so that we can find and delete your Firebase data when you ask. Nothing is sent to us unless you choose to send it: you can include the Support ID in an email, a feedback message or an ad report. We use it only to answer your request, to look up which ad you saw (earlier versions may pass it to LevelPlay Ad Quality) and to delete your data. Resetting or withdrawing does not remove the string from the screen, so you can still ask for deletion afterwards.

Local reminders

If you allow notifications, the Game can schedule local reminders on your device (for example a morning route reminder). These stay on the device; we do not operate a push server for them.

Feedback and ad reports

Send feedback opens an in-game form that posts to a Google Form we operate. You can include a message and an optional email address. The form also receives device and build details (version, model, OS, language) so we can reproduce problems. It may also include the Support ID string. We use feedback only to answer you and improve the Game. Google processes the form submission under its own terms.

Report an ad opens your email app with a pre-filled message to us (including the Support ID string when available). Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email.

3. In-App Purchases and Payments

Morning Paper Route offers optional one-time, non-consumable purchases processed by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details, only whether each purchase is active on your store account:

  • Remove Ads removes the short ads between game days. Optional rewarded videos (revive, extra papers, double coins) stay available.
  • Town packs unlock additional neighborhood routes. Bike and rider cosmetics are still earned through gameplay and in-game coins.

4. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, or physical addresses to play. If you include an email in feedback or email us directly, we receive that address and message only to reply.
  • We do not request access to camera, microphone, or photo library.
  • We do not track precise GPS location.
  • We do not use Firebase with your advertising ID, and we do not use it to show or personalize ads.
  • We do not use Google signals, and all Google Analytics data sharing with Google is switched off.
  • We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Children and Age Rating

Morning Paper Route is a general-audience mobile arcade game made for teens and adults. The Game is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe that an ad provider has collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately and we will coordinate deletion with the provider. The Game does not start Unity Analytics, Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics for a player whom the app store has identified as under 13. From version 1.2.4, for a player identified as 13 to 17, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics start only if that player has turned on "Share gameplay statistics" themselves. Today only Google Play gives the Game this signal; on iPhone and iPad the Game has no age signal. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game, contact us and we will delete what we can and coordinate with the providers.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have legal rights to access, delete, or limit the processing and sharing of your personal data for targeted advertising. Because we do not operate user accounts or our own servers, you can exercise these rights through the Game, your device and the providers, or by contacting us:

  • Adjusting your privacy, ad consent, and "Share gameplay statistics" choices inside the in-game Settings menu;
  • Asking us to delete Firebase data using your Support ID (see Delete Your Data);
  • Resetting or deleting your Advertising ID via your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking);
  • Deleting the application to clear all local device storage;
  • Contacting us at the email address below for any privacy inquiries.

For step-by-step deletion instructions, see Delete Your Data below.

Delete Your Data

To delete your data from Morning Paper Route, use whichever of these applies to you:

  1. Data stored on your device (saves, settings, reminders): delete the app. We do not keep a copy on our servers.
  2. Gameplay statistics and crash reports (if "Share gameplay statistics" was on): turn "Share gameplay statistics" off in Settings. For Unity Analytics this also requests deletion. For Firebase it stops collection and the data expires as described above. To have Firebase data deleted sooner, email aviisr@aviisrgames.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the Support ID shown in Settings > Privacy and data (tap Copy). We delete the Firebase Analytics data for each ID in your Support ID, and the matching rows in our BigQuery export, and reply to confirm. Google processes deletion requests in its own time and we cannot make it faster. Crash reports are not deleted individually: they expire after 90 days.
  3. Feedback you sent (your message, the optional email address, device details and a random support ID): email aviisr@aviisrgames.com with the subject "Delete my data" and tell us which feedback to delete (for example, the email address you wrote in it). We delete that feedback and reply to confirm.
  4. Advertising data held by ad partners: reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (see section 6). If you want us to ask an ad provider to delete data, email us at the address above and we will coordinate with the provider.
  5. Leaderboard scores and purchases are held by Google Play Games or Game Center, and by Google Play or the App Store, under your platform account. Manage or delete them in your Google or Apple account settings.

What is deleted and what is kept: we delete the data described above and keep no copy afterwards. The only records that remain are those a platform or payment provider must keep for legal or accounting reasons, such as purchase records held by Apple or Google.
