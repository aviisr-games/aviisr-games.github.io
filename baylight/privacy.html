Privacy Policy

Baylight: Sort & Ship (Android) · Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Baylight app ("the game", "we") uses and why. Questions: aviisr@gmail.com

Information the game keeps on your device

There are no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress, coins, settings and the level in progress are saved on your device. The game works offline. Deleting the game deletes them.

Game analytics

To understand how levels are played and to improve them, the game sends gameplay events to Unity Analytics, provided by Unity Technologies. The events are: a level starts, a level is completed (with stars, jams and time taken), a level is restarted, and the free undo is used. Unity also records standard usage data such as when the game is opened, with device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and a country derived from your IP address. The events carry no name, email or contact details.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Share usage data. When it is off, the game stops sending events, and it asks Unity to delete the data it holds for your device.

Europe, the UK and Switzerland: on devices in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, this version of the game sends no analytics and nothing to Meta. It decides from your mobile network, SIM or device region; if it cannot tell, it sends nothing.

Install measurement (Android)

On Android, the game reports one "app activation" to Meta (Facebook) each time it starts. This lets Meta count installs that came from our advertising. The game sends Meta no gameplay, level or purchase events. Meta receives your advertising identifier and your IP address with that event. This is not sent when "Share usage data" is off.

Advertising

This version of the game shows no advertising. If that changes, we will update this page before the change ships.

Purchases

This version has no purchases. If purchases are added, they will be processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and we never receive your payment details.

Children

Baylight is a general-audience puzzle game for players 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off usage data in Settings, reset your advertising identifier in your device settings, and delete all data on your device by deleting the game. To ask for access to, or deletion of, data we hold, email aviisr@gmail.com. If you are in the EU, the UK, California or another region with privacy laws, you have rights under those laws, and we will honor them on request.

Changes

If the game's use of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
