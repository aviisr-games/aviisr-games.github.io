This website

  • No cookies, no accounts, no forms. The site loads no third-party fonts, ads or trackers.
  • Visit counting. Some pages include a counter (GoatCounter) that counts visits in total. It records the page address, the referring website, clicks on a Google Play button, and the browser and operating system name (for example Chrome on Android), worked out from the browser header, which itself is not stored. It does not record country, region, language or screen size, does not track visitors across pages or visits, and deletes the data after 400 days. It uses no cookies and no personal identifiers. It does not count visitors who turn on Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
  • Never on Mila pages. The counter never runs on the Mila pages, which are for parents of children, or on privacy and support pages.
  • Language choice. Your language choice is saved in your browser (local storage) so the next page opens in the same language. It never leaves your device.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.
