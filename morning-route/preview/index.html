Each shift increases courier speed, subscriber density, and introduces new neighborhood hazards:

Day 1: Quiet Suburb Warmup
City: San Francisco (Quiet Residential) · Base Speed: 1.0× · Target Subscribers: 15
Active Hazards: 🗑️ Bins

Courier Briefing: Monday morning warmup. Learn the mailbox arc, steer around curbside bins, and establish your first combo multiplier.

The 6 Neighborhood Hazards & Counter-Moves

  • 1. Curbside Bins: Steer with one thumb or bunny-hop curbs. Crashing spills papers.
  • 2. Crosswalk Pedestrians: A tossed newspaper knocks a pedestrian to a halt; steer wide around them.
  • 3. Chasing Dogs: Barking dogs chase from front yards. Tap the Turbo Burst to outrun their sprint.
  • 4. Reversing Cars: Watch for white backup lights in driveways; time your acceleration through the opening.
  • 5. Delivery Drones: Low-flying drones intercept airborne newspapers. Delay your throw until the flightpath clears.
  • 6. Zigzagging Skaters: Skaters swerve unpredictably. Hit them with a newspaper to knock them down or bunny-hop clear.
