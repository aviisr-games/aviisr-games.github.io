About aviisr games

An independent studio making small mobile games, and writing about how they work.

Who we are

aviisr games is a one-person studio run by Avi Israeli. We build mobile games for Android and publish them on Google Play: Ninely (a sudoku game that explains every move), Middle East 2030 (a political strategy simulation set in an invented 2030), Baylight (a one-thumb truck-sorting puzzle), Morning Paper Route (a bike arcade game) and Mila (an English-learning app for children in Hebrew-speaking families).

The games are small on purpose. Each one tries to do one thing well, to start fast and to respect the player's time and privacy. This website is built the same way: plain pages, no accounts, no sign-up forms and a visit counter that adds up totals without following anyone.

What you will find here

  • Game pages. A page for each game, with an honest description, screenshots from the real app and browser versions of some games you can try without installing anything.
  • Sudoku guides. A growing library of step-by-step guides to solving techniques, from naked singles to Swordfish. Every example board is checked by program to have exactly one solution, and every elimination shown in a guide is checked against the board before we publish it.
  • Strategy notes. For Middle East 2030, a guide to the eight capitals, the monthly decisions and the career endings.
  • Printable worksheets. For Mila, free English flashcards and worksheets for primary-school grades, in Hebrew, with answer keys.

How the content is made

The guides are written by us, from how we solve puzzles ourselves and from the solver built into Ninely. We do not copy text from app-store listings or from other websites. When a technique has a standard name, we use it and explain it in our own words, with our own boards.

Middle East 2030 is a work of fiction. No event, headline or leader in it is real, and the game is rated Everyone 10+ on Google Play.

Advertising and privacy

Our games on Google Play are free and may show ads or offer purchases, as described in each game's privacy policy. On this website, some Sudoku guide pages may show ads served by Google AdSense. Games, support pages, privacy pages and everything about Mila are always ad-free. Read the full details in the website privacy policy.
